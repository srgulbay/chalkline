1. The app

Chalkline helps you take job photos, file them to jobs, mark them up, create PDF reports, export your data and, if you choose, keep customer records and send customers updates. There is no account. Your data is stored on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on for the app, in your own iCloud account (see the Privacy Policy: https://srgulbay.github.io/chalkline/privacy/).

2. Free plan and Chalkline Pro

  • The free plan includes up to 3 jobs (archived jobs included). Reports on the free plan carry a small "Made with Chalkline" footer.
  • Chalkline Pro is an auto-renewable subscription, monthly or yearly. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Pro unlocks unlimited jobs, reports without the footer and with your logo and color, same-angle before & after photos, the date and location stamp on shared photos, editable message templates and update suggestions when a job changes stage.
  • Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you buy and can differ between countries.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • If you buy a subscription during a free trial, the rest of the trial ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings. Canceling stops the next renewal; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Apple handles all payments and refunds. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • The subscription is not shared through Family Sharing.
  • If Pro ends, nothing is deleted: all your jobs, photos and reports stay, and you can keep viewing and exporting them. Creating new jobs beyond the free limit and the Pro features need Pro again.

4. Photo evidence

The app records each photo's time from your iPhone's clock, its location from your iPhone's location services and a SHA-256 fingerprint of the original file at the moment of capture. The fingerprint lets you show that an exported original has not changed since then. Location accuracy, addresses (looked up through Apple's services) and the clock depend on your device and its settings. We cannot guarantee that a court, insurer or other party will accept your photos or reports as evidence.
