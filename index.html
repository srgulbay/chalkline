Chalkline Support

Chalkline is an iPhone app for tradespeople who document their jobs with photos: before and after photos, PDF reports and customer updates.

Contact us

Questions, problems or ideas? Email us at srgulbay@gmail.com and we will reply. It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the app version (shown in the app's Settings).

Chalkline Pro subscription

  • Chalkline Pro is sold and billed by Apple through your Apple Account.
  • To change or cancel your subscription, follow Apple's guide: Cancel a subscription from Apple. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings.
  • New iPhone or reinstalled the app? Tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings.
  • Apple handles all payments and refunds. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
