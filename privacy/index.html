Chalkline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Chalkline is an iPhone app for tradespeople who document their jobs with photos. It is published by Sait Ramazan Gülbay ("we", "us"). In Turkish, the same app is called Ustaca. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • Your photos, jobs, customers and reports stay on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud for the app, in your own iCloud account. We cannot see or access them.

Information you store in the app

You decide what goes into Chalkline. This can include:

  • job photos, with the date, time, time zone, location and address where each photo was taken, a SHA-256 fingerprint of the original, and your captions, tags and markup;
  • jobs: title, address, location, stage, dates and notes;
  • customers: name, phone number, email address, postal address, message language, notes and the date a customer agreed to receive updates;
  • your company profile: company and owner name, phone, email, registration number, trade, logo and color;
  • reports, the job timeline (including the text of updates you sent to customers) and your message templates.

This information is stored on your iPhone. It is never sent to us.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on for Chalkline in the iOS Settings app, the app syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit to the private database of your own iCloud account, so it is available on your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is stored and protected by Apple under the iCloud terms and Apple's Privacy Policy. Developers have no access to the private iCloud data of their users. If iCloud is off, everything stays on your iPhone and the app still works fully.

Permissions

Chalkline asks for a permission only when you first use the feature that needs it. You can change your choice at any time in the iOS Settings app.

  • Camera: to take job photos.
  • Location (While Using the App): only while the camera is open or when you tap "Use Current Location" in a job. The location is saved with your photo and used on your iPhone to find the job you are at. The app never uses your location in the background.
  • Add to Photos: only to save photos you choose to export to your photo library. The app cannot read your photo library. When you pick a logo, you choose a single image in the system picker.
  • Contacts: the app does not ask for access to your contacts. When you add a customer from Contacts, the system contact picker gives the app only the one contact you select.

Services the app uses for you

  • Addresses (Apple Maps services): to turn a photo's coordinates into a street address, the app sends the coordinates to Apple's geocoding service. Apple processes this request under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive nothing.
  • Purchases (App Store): subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your subscription status on your iPhone.
  • Messages: when you send an update to a customer, the app opens WhatsApp, Messages or Mail with the text filled in, and you send it yourself. Those messages are handled by those apps under their own terms. Chalkline never sends messages in the background.
  • Sharing: reports, photos and exports leave the app only when you share them through the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you choose.
  • Spotlight: customer names and contact details can appear in iPhone search. This index stays on your iPhone. You can turn it off in the app under Settings.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in the iOS Settings app to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymized crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple controls this feature and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. The app itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting code.

Your customers' data

The customer details you enter belong to your business. Under data protection laws you are responsible for them (for example as the "controller" under the GDPR, the "veri sorumlusu" under KVKK or the "controlador" under LGPD). We do not access or process this data on your behalf. Please ask your customers before you send them updates; the app lets you record the date they agreed. You can export your data or delete a customer at any time; deleting a customer removes their personal details and the texts of updates sent to them.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete single photos, jobs and customers, or use Settings → Delete All Data in the app. When iCloud is on, deletions also apply to your iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone; data in iCloud can be managed in the iOS Settings app under your Apple Account → iCloud. Before deleting, you can export everything as a ZIP file.

Security

Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS data protection and your passcode. Apple encrypts iCloud data in transit and on its servers.

Your rights and regional notes

Because we do not collect or store any of your personal data, we have nothing to give you access to, correct, delete, transfer or sell. You keep full control in the app and in your Apple Account.

  • European Union and United Kingdom (GDPR, UK GDPR): we do not process your personal data as a controller or processor. Apple is responsible for App Store and iCloud data.
  • Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698): we do not process your personal data as a data controller.
  • Brazil (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018): we do not process your personal data as a controller or operator.
  • California (CCPA as amended by the CPRA): we do not collect, sell or share personal information and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

If you have a question or a request, email us and we will reply.

Children

Chalkline is a work tool for adults. It is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your data, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
