Chalkline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Chalkline is an iPhone app for tradespeople who document their jobs with photos. It is published by Sait Ramazan Gülbay ("we", "us"). In Turkish, the same app is called Ustaca. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

The short version

  • The app does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. If you email us, we receive only what you send (see "If you email us").
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • Your photos, jobs, customers and reports stay on your iPhone and, if you turn on iCloud for the app, in your own iCloud account. We cannot see or access them.

Information you store in the app

You decide what goes into Chalkline. This can include:

  • job photos, with the date, time, time zone, location and address where each photo was taken, a SHA-256 fingerprint of the original, and your captions, tags and markup;
  • jobs: title, address, location, stage, dates and notes;
  • customers: name, phone number, email address, postal address, message language, notes and the date a customer agreed to receive updates;
  • your company profile: company and owner name, phone, email, registration number, trade, logo and color;
  • reports, the job timeline (including the text of updates you sent to customers) and your message templates.

This information is stored on your iPhone. It is never sent to us.

iCloud sync

If iCloud is turned on for Chalkline in the iOS Settings app, the app syncs your data through Apple's CloudKit to the private database of your own iCloud account, so it is available on your other iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is stored and protected by Apple under the iCloud terms and Apple's Privacy Policy. Developers have no access to the private iCloud data of their users. If iCloud is off, everything stays on your iPhone and the app still works fully.

Permissions

Chalkline asks for a permission only when you first use the feature that needs it. You can change your choice at any time in the iOS Settings app.

  • Camera: to take job photos.
  • Location (While Using the App): only while the camera is open or when you tap "Use Current Location" in a job. The location is saved with your photo and used on your iPhone to find the job you are at. The app never uses your location in the background.
  • Add to Photos: only to save photos you choose to export to your photo library. The app cannot read your photo library. When you pick a logo, you choose a single image in the system picker.
  • Contacts: the app does not ask for access to your contacts. When you add a customer from Contacts, the system contact picker gives the app only the one contact you select.

Services the app uses for you

  • Addresses (Apple Maps services): to turn a photo's coordinates into a street address, the app sends the coordinates to Apple's geocoding service. Apple processes this request under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive nothing.
  • Purchases (App Store): subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your subscription status on your iPhone.
  • Messages, calls and directions: when you send an update to a customer, the app opens WhatsApp, Messages or Mail (or your default email app if Mail has no account) with the text filled in, and you send it yourself. On a customer's card, Call opens the Phone app with the customer's number and Directions opens Apple Maps with the customer's address. Those apps handle your messages, calls and routes under their own terms. Chalkline never sends messages or makes calls in the background.
  • Sharing: reports, photos and exports leave the app only when you share them through the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you choose. Check what a file contains before you share it: original photos keep their EXIF data, including the GPS location and the date and time they were taken; stamped copies (Pro) show the date, time and address on the image; reports show the job address and each photo's date, time and address; a ZIP export contains the original photos and a metadata.json file with the details of the job's customer or, when you export all data, of all your customers (name, phone, email, address and notes).
  • Spotlight: so you can find customers when you search your iPhone, the app adds each customer's name, phone number, email address and postal address to the search index on your iPhone. Jobs and notes are not added. The index stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us. This setting is on by default; turn off "Show Customers in Spotlight" in the app's Settings to remove your customers from the index.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and controls: the "Take Job Photo" action works with Siri, the Shortcuts app, Spotlight, the Action button and a Control Center or Lock Screen control. When you choose a job for a shortcut, the Shortcuts app shows the titles and addresses of your jobs, read from your iPhone; they are not sent to us. The action only opens the camera in the app. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive nothing.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in the iOS Settings app to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymized crash reports and usage statistics with us. Apple controls this feature and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. The app itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting code.

If you email us

If you write to our support address, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, your message and any attachments. We use them only to answer you (our legitimate interest in replying: GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f), LGPD Art. 7(IX)). We keep them only as long as needed for that, longer only where the law requires it, and we delete them when you ask. The emails are kept by our email provider (Google Gmail), which may store them on servers outside your country. For these emails we are the controller, and you can ask us for access, correction or deletion. Writing to us happens outside the app, so the app's App Store privacy label, "Data Not Collected", stays correct. Please do not send customer details or photos unless we need them to solve your problem.

Your customers' data

The customer details you enter belong to your business. Under data protection laws you are responsible for them (for example as the "controller" under the GDPR, the "veri sorumlusu" under KVKK or the "controlador" under LGPD). We do not access or process this data on your behalf. Please ask your customers before you send them updates; the app lets you record the date they agreed. You can export your data or delete a customer at any time; deleting a customer removes their personal details and the texts of updates sent to them. If iCloud sync is on, Apple stores this data in your iCloud account and may keep it on servers in other countries, including the United States. As the controller, you decide whether this suits your business and inform your customers where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete single photos, jobs and customers, or use Settings → Delete All Data in the app. When iCloud is on, deletions also apply to your iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your iPhone; data in iCloud can be managed in the iOS Settings app under your Apple Account → iCloud. Before deleting, you can export everything as a ZIP file.

Security

Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS data protection and your passcode. Apple encrypts iCloud data in transit and on its servers.

Your rights and regional notes

Apart from the emails you send us (see "If you email us"), we do not collect or store any of your personal data, so we have nothing to give you access to, correct, delete, transfer or sell. You keep full control in the app and in your Apple Account.

  • European Union and United Kingdom (GDPR, UK GDPR): we do not process personal data from the app as a controller or processor; we are the controller only for emails you send us. Apple is responsible for App Store and iCloud data.
  • Türkiye (KVKK, Law No. 6698): we do not process personal data from the app as a data controller; we are the data controller only for emails you send us.
  • Brazil (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018): we do not process personal data from the app as a controller or operator; we are the controller only for emails you send us.
  • California (CCPA as amended by the CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and the app collects none.

If you have a question or a request, email us and we will reply.

Children

Chalkline is a work tool for adults. It is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects what happens to your data, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
